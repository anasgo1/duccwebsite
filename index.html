About The Clinic

Reliable, patient-focused care for families across the GTA.

Doctors Urgent Care Clinic provides family medicine and urgent care with a focus on professionalism, patient comfort, and clear medical guidance. Since 2012, the clinic has supported patients through organized, respectful, and accessible care.

Every part of the experience is designed to feel calm and dependable, from the first call to the visit, assessment, treatment plan, and follow-up when needed.
